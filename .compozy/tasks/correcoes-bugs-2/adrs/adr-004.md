# ADR-004: Faltantes Tab Icon — 🔍 (Magnifying Glass)

**Date:** 2026-06-03
**Status:** Accepted

## Decision

Replace the ❌ emoji on the Faltantes tab with 🔍 (magnifying glass).

## Alternatives Considered

- **❓ (question mark):** Implies uncertainty rather than active search.
- **📝 (notepad):** Represents a list, not the act of finding missing stickers.

## Consequences

- 🔍 communicates "searching for missing stickers" — matches the screen's purpose.
- One-line change in `RootNavigator.tsx` `tabIcons` map.
