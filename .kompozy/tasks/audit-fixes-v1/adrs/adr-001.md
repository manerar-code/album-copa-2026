# ADR-001: Three-Phase Rollout for Audit Fixes

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-06-08

## Context

A full technical audit of the Álbum Copa 2026 app identified 31 issues across four severity levels (3 critical, 11 high, 12 medium, 5 low). The app currently shows a black screen on iOS TestFlight across 6 consecutive builds, blocking all validation and store submission. The team needs to decide how to sequence the remediation work across a single PRD.

## Decision

Adopt a three-phase rollout:
- **Phase 1 — iOS Black Screen:** Fix the 6 root causes of the black screen (CRITICAL-01/02/03 + HIGH-01/02/03) and validate on TestFlight before proceeding.
- **Phase 2 — Store Readiness:** Fix remaining HIGH items (HIGH-04 to HIGH-11) and store-critical MEDIUM items (MEDIUM-07/08/09) to enable App Store and Google Play submission.
- **Phase 3 — Quality & Polish:** Address remaining MEDIUM and LOW items as continuous improvement after store submission.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative B: Big Bang — All 31 items in one release

- **Description**: Implement all fixes before any build or submission.
- **Pros**: Single release cycle, all issues resolved together.
- **Cons**: High risk — if black screen persists, all other effort is wasted. Long cycle without intermediate validation.
- **Why rejected**: No early feedback loop; black screen could persist through all work.

### Alternative C: Four phases by severity (Critical → High → Medium → Low)

- **Description**: Four separate phases aligned to severity levels, each with its own build.
- **Pros**: Very granular progress tracking.
- **Cons**: Unnecessary split between Critical and High items that share the same root cause (black screen). Four builds instead of three.
- **Why rejected**: Over-granular; adds build overhead without meaningful benefit over three phases.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Phase 1 provides early validation of the black screen fix before investing in store readiness work.
- Phase 2 enables store submission with a clean, stable build.
- Phase 3 items do not block launch and can be released as a patch update.

### Negative

- Store submission is gated on Phase 2 completion, not Phase 1.
- Sign in with Apple (MEDIUM-07) is required for App Store but lands in Phase 2, not Phase 1.

### Risks

- If Phase 1 does not resolve the black screen, Phase 2 and 3 timelines shift.
- Mitigation: Phase 1 is scoped to the minimum viable fix set; can be iterated quickly.
