# ADR-001: Bug Fix Delivery Strategy — Big Bang

**Date:** 2026-06-02
**Status:** Accepted

## Context

The Álbum Copa 2026 app has 10 identified bugs spanning visual layout, business logic, and iOS rendering. A decision was needed on how to group and deliver the fixes.

## Decision

All 10 bugs will be corrected in a single development cycle and delivered as one build to TestFlight.

## Alternatives Considered

- **Phased delivery (3 phases):** Lower risk, incremental TestFlight validation, but longer time to full resolution.
- **Critical bugs only (MVP):** Fastest initial delivery, but leaves functional bugs unresolved indefinitely.

## Consequences

- All fixes are validated together before a new TestFlight build is submitted.
- If a build issue arises, all fixes are delayed together.
- Simpler tracking — one cycle, one build, one submit.
