# ADR-004: FIXED_TYPES Visibility in TypeSettingsModal

**Date:** 2026-06-02
**Status:** Accepted

## Decision

Render Player, Brilhante (Foil Player), and Silver at the top of the TypeSettingsModal list with a checked, disabled checkbox and a lock icon (🔒). They are not toggleable.

## Alternatives Considered

- **Keep invisible:** Types are enforced by the store but not shown. Users have no awareness of why certain stickers always appear, which creates confusion.

## Consequences

- Users understand which types are mandatory and why.
- No new store logic required; `FIXED_TYPES` already enforced in `userSettingsStore.ts`.
- Requires UI-only change in `TypeSettingsModal.tsx`: render a separate locked section above configurable types.
